Install Meditation Coach

Add Meditation Coach to your Home Screen to use it full-screen, offline, and ad-free just like a native app.

  1. Open Safari on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Tap the Share button ⎋ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add in the top-right corner.
  1. Open Chrome on your Android device.
  2. Tap the Menu icon (3 dots) ⋮ at the top-right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm by tapping Install.
  1. Open the app in Chrome or Edge.
  2. Tap the Install icon ⊕ in the URL bar at the top-right.
  3. Confirm by clicking Install.
  4. The app will run in its own dedicated, clean window.
